Your home visit, step by step

Whether your cat dislikes the carrier or your older dog finds travel difficult, we’ll start by discussing what they need.

  1. 01

    Tell me about your pet

    Send your address, your pet’s species and age, and a description of their symptoms or the care you’re looking for. Mention any worries about handling or previous vet visits.

  2. 02

    Agree a plan

    I’ll check whether care at home is suitable and discuss the appointment, preparation and planned price. Some conditions need a clinic’s equipment.

  3. 03

    Care in familiar surroundings

    Have your pet’s records and medication list ready. During the visit, I’ll explain the findings and discuss any recommended tests, treatment and next steps.

Before you book

Can the whole visit be in English?

Yes. We can discuss your pet’s history, the examination and the next steps in English. You can also send your appointment request in English.

My cat is frightened of vets. Can you help?

A home visit removes the journey and waiting room, but the examination itself can still be stressful. Tell me how your cat reacts to strangers and handling so we can discuss a suitable approach. Only give calming medication if agreed with a vet beforehand.

Can you visit an older dog or cat?

Yes. Health checks, blood sampling, ultrasound and palliative care may be available at home. We’ll discuss which care is appropriate for your pet before arranging a visit.

What is included in the price?

Treatment and travel are charged separately. Some medicines are charged by the quantity used, which depends on your pet’s species, weight and the medicine chosen. Before the visit, we’ll discuss the planned care, any examination fee and the expected cost.

Should I prepare anything?

Have your pet’s vaccination record, previous test results and medication list ready. Choose a quiet room. Ask about preparation for blood tests or ultrasound; do not withhold food or give calming medication without prior advice.

Is this an emergency service?

Home visits are by arrangement and depend on availability; this is not a 24-hour emergency service. If your pet is seriously unwell or deteriorating, seek urgent veterinary help rather than waiting for a WhatsApp reply.
