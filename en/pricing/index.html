How travel is calculated

The starting call-out fee is 420 CZK. In Prague 2, Prague 3, Prague 7, Prague 8 and Benešov, no kilometres are charged and only the starting fee applies. Elsewhere, the one-way Google Maps distance from the nearer base is charged in both directions at 20 CZK per kilometre each way.

This page is an indicative price guide, not a final quote. The exact service, availability, any extra charges and total price are confirmed before the visit.
